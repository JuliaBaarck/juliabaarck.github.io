About

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at LMU Munich and a doctoral researcher at the ifo Institute, where I am part of the ifo Center for Public Finance. I expect to complete my PhD in 2027.

My research is in public and labor economics. I study how inequality is transmitted across generations, what shapes people’s views about fairness and redistribution, and how institutions affect the careers people end up in. I work primarily with large-scale administrative and survey data.

Before starting my PhD I earned an M.Sc. in Econometrics and Empirical Economics from the Toulouse School of Economics and a B.Sc. in Economics from the University of Mannheim. I have visited the University of California, San Diego and the Aix-Marseille School of Economics.
